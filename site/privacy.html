Privacy

FRIDAY's browser extension. Updated 23 November 2026.

What the extension reads

When you ask FRIDAY about the page you're on, the extension reads the tab in front: its title, its address, any text you have selected and the page's text. It reads nothing else, never on its own, and never from a private window.

Where it goes

The extension hands what it read to the FRIDAY app on the same computer, through the browser's native messaging. The extension itself sends nothing to any server.

FRIDAY adds it to your question and sends both to the AI you chose in FRIDAY: its free default models, a provider you gave your own key for, or a model running on your computer. FRIDAY doesn't keep the page; its record of what it sent where is on your computer, in FRIDAY's egress log.

What isn't collected

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data. FRIDAY's makers receive nothing from the extension.

Questions

FRIDAY is open source: github.com/sreevarshan-xenoz/project-friday. Ask in its issues.
